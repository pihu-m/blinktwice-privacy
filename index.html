BlinkTwice for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

BlinkTwice does not collect any data. It never connects to the internet, and nothing you do in it leaves your Mac.

No network access

BlinkTwice runs in the macOS App Sandbox without network permission, so macOS itself blocks any connection. There are no analytics, crash reporters, ads, accounts, tracking or update checks.

Camera (optional)

The camera is off until you turn on “Use camera” in the Welcome window or in Settings.

  • It runs only while a break’s countdown is running. The camera light is on only then.
  • Each frame is checked on your Mac, using Apple’s on-device Vision framework, for one thing: whether your eyes look open. Then the frame is discarded.
  • No photo, video or frame is ever saved, shown or sent anywhere.

You can turn the camera off at any time in BlinkTwice Settings, or revoke access in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera.

Microphone and calls

To avoid starting a break during a call, BlinkTwice asks macOS whether a microphone or camera is in use by any app. It only sees “in use” or “not in use”. It never listens to or records audio and has no microphone access.

Activity

To count screen time, BlinkTwice asks macOS how many seconds have passed since your last keyboard or mouse input. It never sees what you type or click.

Your images and settings

Images you add to a custom Cover-up are resized and stored only inside BlinkTwice’s own container on your Mac (~/Library/Containers/com.mohitmehta.BlinkTwice/). Your originals are never changed. Your preferences are stored locally and are not synced or sent anywhere.

Children

BlinkTwice collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. A future version of the app will never start collecting data without this policy being updated first.
